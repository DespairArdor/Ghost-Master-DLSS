Adds NVIDIA DLSS Super Resolution and DLSS Frame Generation (2x) to Ghost Master: Resurrection through a ReShade add-on. In-game settings tab for render resolution, DLSS preset and Frame Generation. Requires ReShade with full add-on support and an RTX GPU (Frame Generation: RTX 40/50).
